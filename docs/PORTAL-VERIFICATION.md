# Portal verification

## Designated family recipient and deliberate recovery — 11 October 2026

The current policy delivers a self-service receipt only to its original authenticated uploader. For staff-assisted payments, it uses the selected family adult's stored authorized account. Invalid or inactive contact data can hold email without undoing bank confirmation or the immutable receipt. Missing accounts or required family links still fail closed. No form can supply an arbitrary or student email address.

An administrator can deliberately prepare the first email after repairing a held recipient. Preparation rechecks authorization, active family links, the frozen recipient ID, valid current email, verified payment and issued receipt. It atomically queues one message and audit entry, without sending. Concurrent/lost-response retries return the same preparation. Existing email history cannot be retargeted or revived; annulled receipts cannot use this action.
