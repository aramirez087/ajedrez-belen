# Ajedrez Belén — “Portada”, a chess magazine for a local club

The landing page is set like a chess publication. Chess has a long print culture — magazine covers, newspaper columns, diagram books — and the page borrows its forms to feel crafted and specific to the club rather than like a generic template. The approved wooden knight is the cover star; the boards are drawn as printed diagrams; the page ends on a red back cover that asks “¿Jugamos?”.

## Concept, section by section

1. **Cover (`#inicio`).** A Bodoni masthead, “Ajedrez”, spans the frame. The wooden knight stands in front of it with its ears tucked into the bottom of the letters, as the subject of a magazine cover does, while the whole word stays legible. An issue line (place · club · minimum age) sits above, and three cover lines (monthly fee, ages, free first class) run down the right column. The headline, intro and WhatsApp button sit bottom left. The knight is static, as previously requested; only the masthead wipes up behind it and the copy fades in.
2. **Índice.** A table of contents strip whose top rule is the floor the knight stands on. It is the section navigation on phones, where the header keeps only the WhatsApp button.
3. **01 Clases y mensualidades (`#niveles`, `#mensualidades`).** “De peón a dama”: the three levels climb a staircase, each on a hatched dark square with a white piece (pawn, rook, queen). The squares are b2, d4 and f6 on the dark long diagonal, and on desktop a red diagram arrow draws itself along that diagonal through c3 and e5 when the section appears. The tiles share one width and one rise, so their centres are collinear; CSS trigonometry (`atan2`, `hypot`, `cos`) sets the arrow's angle and length from the container width, and browsers without those functions simply omit it. Prices align across the three columns for comparison. Then a help line, venue/schedule/first-class facts, a dashed “coupon” for the 25 % family discount and the payment disclosure.
4. **02 El reto (`#reto`).** A newspaper chess column: double rule, sticky copy with a drop cap, and the playable mate-in-one diagram. As in print, the solution is printed upside down under the board and turns the right way up when the problem is solved or revealed.
5. **03 La partida (`#partida`).** A dark, stage-lit spread for Morphy's Opera Game (1858). The diagram is a paper card on the dark ground; the scrollytelling logic is unchanged. Below it, the complete scoresheet (planilla) in Spanish notation marks the five positions shown on the board, so the story ends with the whole game and its 1–0.
6. **04 El club (`#asociacion`).** Association copy, a three-photo story of concentration, achievement and friendship in full color, the three principles (Jugar, Pensar, Compartir) with Burnett glyphs, and the purpose statement as a large italic pull quote with Misión/Visión disclosures. Staggered desktop photographs become a simple vertical story on phones. A separate team portrait feature follows classes/prices, introducing the people before the interactive chess sections while keeping practical information first.
7. **05 Preguntas (`#preguntas`).** A sticky heading beside a single column of disclosures.
8. **¿Jugamos? (`#consultas`, `#ubicacion`).** The back cover: crest red, the crest's three gold stars, a giant italic “¿Jugamos?”, the three steps to a first class, the main WhatsApp button and practical details.

## Visual system

- **Colour.** Bone paper `#f3eee3`, newsprint `#ebe4d4`, ink `#16130f`, and crest red `#b3222b` (`#8e1a21` on hover). Supporting text uses `--muted` `#655d50` (5.6:1 on paper). On ink, secondary text is `--on-ink` `#cdc4b2` and the accent is `--red-bright` `#ec6a62` (5.5:1); on red, `--on-red` `#f8ddd8` (5.2:1). The stars use `--gold` `#d3a24a`, decoratively only. A light SVG grain gives paper and ink surfaces a printed texture. Every colour is a `:root` token in `src/styles/landing.css`.
- **Type.** Bodoni Moda (variable, optical sizes 6–96) for display, prices and headings, with italics for emphasis in red. DM Sans for reading and controls. DM Mono 500 for labels, notation and captions, set in tracked capitals. Visible labels have a 12 px floor (11 px for two space-limited labels on phones); only the decorative diagram coordinates are smaller.
- **Fonts, built for this page.** `scripts/build-fonts.py` trims Bodoni Moda and DM Sans to the weights and optical sizes the pages use and to the Spanish character set (Bodoni roman 46 → 34 KB, italic 54 → 39 KB, DM Sans 37 → 21 KB). It also adds the one kerning pair the display face lacks: Bodoni's U ends in a hairline stem, so “Un tablero” and “Una” read as “U n” at display sizes; U before a stemmed lowercase letter now closes up by 0.04 em. A coverage check confirmed every character rendered on the seven styled pages exists in the trimmed fonts.
- **The colón.** Neither Bodoni Moda nor DM Sans has `₡`, so prices previously fell back to a system font. The same script draws the sign from each family's own C and two diagonal strokes; the results (`public/fonts/*-colon.woff2`, under 0.5 KB each) are served with `unicode-range: U+20A1`.
- **Diagrams.** Boards are printed diagrams: paper light squares, dark squares hatched at 45°, a double ink frame and coordinates outside the board. Pieces are Colin M.L. Burnett's set from one inline sprite (`ChessPieceSprite.astro`, used through `ChessPiece.astro`).
- **Shape.** Pill buttons with a round WhatsApp badge mark every link that opens WhatsApp. Sections are numbered with a small ink square (a board square) beside a mono label.
- **Layout.** A 1360 px frame with `clamp()` gutters. The cover is a container: the masthead is sized in `cqi` so it fills the frame, capped by viewport height, and `--mast` also sets the copy's clearance so the H1 never collides with the descender of the j. Between 960 and 1199 px the cover tightens; below 960 px it stacks with the knight overlapping the masthead.
- **Knight and fold.** The knight is an absolutely positioned grid child whose box runs from below the masthead to the cover's bottom edge, extended upward by 0.27 × the masthead size. Its ear tips therefore land 0.17–0.19 em above the baseline at every desktop size (0.18–0.23 em when stacked), instead of climbing up to 0.6 em into the word on tall screens as a percentage height did; it never exceeds 780 px. The cover fills the screen below the header up to 960 px. On screens tall enough to show the Índice as well (over about 1115 px), it grows instead, so the Índice ends exactly at the fold rather than leaving the next section's top margin as a blank band (cover capped at 1200 px). There the knight stops at its cap and its ears settle on the baseline. The script measures the header and Índice into `--header-offset` and `--contents-h` before first paint; the CSS fallbacks are within 1 px.
- **Light only.** The palette is the identity, so the pages declare `color-scheme: only light`. Without it, Chrome's automatic dark mode repainted the cover dark and the black header button nearly disappeared; with it, the page keeps its paper and ink.

## Motion and interaction

Motion is limited to the cover entrance (CSS only, so content never depends on scripts), reveal-on-scroll, the ladder arrow drawing itself, a scroll-linked lift of the masthead where `animation-timeline` is supported (it moves up, away from the headline, never toward it), and the existing puzzle and Opera transitions. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes all of it. Without JavaScript every section remains visible and the puzzle shows its solution in a `noscript` block.

The header navigation marks the section being read (`aria-current="location"`) and clears the mark on the cover and the closing section. The phone WhatsApp bar appears when the hero button is out of view; it yields to the closing section, which has its own invitation, and to the Opera story, where the pinned board already takes the top of the screen. The header's WhatsApp button stays visible throughout. On pointer devices, puzzle squares show a hover outline and legal targets grow under the cursor. The header measures itself into `--header-offset` for sticky offsets and scroll padding; `--header-h` remains a fixed layout value.

## Accessibility

A skip link, a visible focus ring adapted to each surface, native `details` disclosures, and 44 px minimum targets. The masthead is decorative (`aria-hidden`); the H1 is the headline. The puzzle remains a labelled grid with one tab stop, arrow-key navigation and live status messages. The upside-down solution is hidden from assistive technology because the status message announces the solution. In the scoresheet, highlighted moves are a filled box (not colour alone) and carry visually hidden “(destacada)” text. WhatsApp links carry visually hidden “(WhatsApp)” text or an `aria-label` that contains the visible label.

## Scope

`src/styles/fonts.css` holds the brand `@font-face` rules for every styled page. `src/styles/landing.css` styles the landing page (both inlined) and `LegalPage.astro` (Términos, Privacidad, Datos, Política, Familias, Aplicación). The portal keeps its layouts in `global.css` and `portal.css`; `src/styles/portal-brand.css`, loaded after them by `PortalLayout.astro`, swaps in the same fonts, colours, light-only scheme and pill buttons by overriding their tokens, so no portal markup or flow changed. The internal prototype is untouched. `/app/` and `/privacy/` stay deliberately plain for Google's OAuth review: they keep the same crest, app name, URLs and content. Google's brand verification covers the consent screen (name, logo, home and privacy URLs, domains), not page styling, so this restyle needs no re-verification as long as those stay as they are.
