Chess piece artwork (Staunton set, "cburnett")

Author: Colin M.L. Burnett (Wikimedia Commons user Cburnett), 2006.
Source files (Wikimedia Commons, "SVG chess pieces/Standard transparent"):
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Chess_klt45.svg  (and _qlt45, _rlt45, _blt45, _nlt45, _plt45,
  _kdt45, _qdt45, _rdt45, _bdt45, _ndt45, _pdt45)
Retrieved: 17 October 2026.

The author released these files under a choice of licenses: GFDL (with CC BY-SA 3.0 migration),
BSD and GPL ({{self|GFDL|migration=relicense|BSD|GPL}} on each file page). Ajedrez Belén uses them
under the BSD license (3-clause, the Wikimedia Commons {{BSD}} default), reproduced below.

Modifications: the twelve drawings are combined into one inline SVG <symbol> sprite
(src/components/ChessPieceSprite.astro). Path data was rewritten in equivalent shorter form and
black/white were replaced by currentColor and the --piece-light CSS custom property so the site
can tint the pieces. The shapes are unchanged.
