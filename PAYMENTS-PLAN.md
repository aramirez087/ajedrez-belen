# Payment portal implementation plan

Implementation plan, updated 10 October 2026. The user selected Brevo, approved the compatibility prototype, and then authorized the full portal. The local MVP now implements family links and invitations, monthly charges, submission/correction, independent bank verification, partial allocations and credit, immutable numbered receipts, audited voids, a durable email outbox, administrative exports, upload-first owned drafts, optional reading suggestions and private image/PDF previews. Discounts remain manual. The public site already runs on Cloudflare and a Brevo account exists; this local portal work provisioned no new cloud resources. See the focused [setup handoff](docs/SETUP-HANDOFF.md) for the current account evidence and pending owner actions. See [portal setup](docs/PORTAL-SETUP.md), [operations](docs/PORTAL-OPERATIONS.md), and the earlier [compatibility results](docs/COMPATIBILITY-PROTOTYPE.md). Real WorkOS login/MFA, Brevo delivery, actual Groq recognition/account access, deployed CPU limits and a restore drill remain launch checks.

The association needs a parent portal for submitting bank transfer evidence, an administrator workflow for checking the bank account, and numbered association receipts after verification. The user confirmed 50 families expected, 100 maximum; monthly fees payable within the first eight days; athlete names should appear in the bank transfer description; and association receipts, without Hacienda electronic invoicing in this scope.

The existing project is Astro 7.3.8; the public page remains prerendered. Its README records fees of CRC 12,000 and CRC 15,000 and a 25% sibling discount whose exact conditions are not yet specified. Preserve that distinction: rates are known, the discount calculation is not.

**Selected architecture for validation.** Keep Astro for the public site and add authenticated portal/admin routes using its Cloudflare adapter. Use Cloudflare Workers for server routes, D1 for relational records, private R2 Standard storage for uploaded slips, WorkOS AuthKit for adult login, and Brevo for receipt email. Use server-managed sessions. Children have records but no login accounts. Keep email behind a small provider adapter so changing the sender does not change payment or receipt logic.

Development requires Node >=22.13.0 for the PDF.js client preview dependency. SQLite is appropriate for this workload. D1 supplies managed storage with SQLite SQL semantics. Bun can be an optional local package manager/test runner; the deployed server runs Cloudflare's workerd runtime and accesses D1 through its binding, not through `bun:sqlite`. A Bun server with a SQLite file is also viable, but requires a durable host, disk backup, and operational ownership. D1 avoids having to solve that hosting problem for this small application. [D1 overview](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/), [Astro Cloudflare adapter](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/integrations-guide/cloudflare/).

**Free-tier budget.** These are published allowances checked on 10 October 2026; recheck before provisioning. Account-wide usage from other projects also matters.

| Component | Published allowance / constraint | Design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workers Free | 100,000 requests/day; 10 ms CPU per invocation | Keep routes small; measure authentication, approval, and receipt rendering early. |
| D1 Free | 5 million rows read/day; 100,000 written/day; 500 MB per database, 5 GB total | Index normal queries; keep files outside the database. |
| R2 Standard | 10 GB-month storage; 1 million Class A and 10 million Class B operations/month | Private uploads, file-size and account quotas, and storage monitoring. |
| WorkOS AuthKit | First 1 million monthly active users free; email codes, social login and MFA included | Hosted auth domain and default auth email sender; avoid paid custom auth domain/add-ons. |
| Brevo Free | 300 email sends/day; unused allowance does not roll over | Durable email outbox with throttling and retry; portal receipt available immediately. Free emails include Brevo branding. |

[Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/), [D1 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/pricing/), [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/), [R2 pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/), [WorkOS pricing](https://workos.com/pricing), [Brevo free limits](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208580669-FAQs-What-are-the-limits-of-the-Free-plan).

For an illustrative 100 monthly transfers, one receipt sent separately to two authorized adults means 200 receipt emails/month. If all are approved on one day, Brevo's published daily allowance accommodates that batch with 100 sends left for other messages. A family with separate payments can produce more transfers than this illustration. Authentication emails are handled by WorkOS rather than consuming the application's receipt-email allowance. Still retain an application-owned outbox: quotas, provider activation and delivery failures can delay email independently of payment approval.

Email alternatives checked: Mailjet allows 6,000/month with 200/day; SMTP2GO allows 1,000/month with 200/day; Resend allows 3,000/month with 100/day. Brevo has the most comfortable daily allowance of these options for a batch of 100 receipts sent to two adults. Its tradeoff is a required Sent with Brevo footer on free emails. Confirm that appearance with a sample email before launch. Verify/authenticate the sending domain and complete transactional-platform activation as part of the technical spike; support activation may be needed. Use the provider's HTTPS API from Workers and disable unnecessary open/click tracking. Do not automatically fail over between providers after an ambiguous send, which can duplicate receipts. [Mailjet limits](https://documentation.mailjet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043048393-What-is-this-200-emails-per-day-limit-on-free-accounts), [SMTP2GO limits](https://support.smtp2go.com/hc/en-gb/articles/223087947-Free-Plan), [Resend limits](https://resend.com/docs/knowledge-base/account-quotas-and-limits), [Brevo setup/activation](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000188150-Troubleshooting-Issues-with-Brevo-SMTP), [Brevo API](https://developers.brevo.com/docs/send-a-transactional-email).

At an illustrative average 500 KB per slip, 100 transfers/month add approximately 600 MB/year. At the proposed 3 MB upload limit, that becomes 3.6 GB/year. Stored evidence accumulates: monitor actual totals and agree retention/archive practices before reaching capacity; never silently delete financial history to stay free. Backup copies also count wherever they are stored.

The target is $0 monthly service usage at this scale, not an unconditional zero-spend guarantee. R2 is metered beyond its included allowance and requires subscription checkout; production WorkOS may require a payment method. Plan to authenticate the association's sending domain; domain registration/renewal is separate. WorkOS's paid custom authentication domain is unnecessary for the application's own domain. Use free plans, avoid paid add-ons, alert well before limits, enforce upload/request budgets, and defer emails when the daily quota is exhausted. These controls reduce overage risk; they are not a provider-wide hard billing cap. [R2 setup](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/get-started/), [WorkOS pricing](https://workos.com/pricing), [Brevo sender/domain setup](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/7924908994450-Send-transactional-emails-using-Brevo-SMTP).

**Parent experience.** The administrator creates/enrolls the athlete and invites authorized adults. Each adult signs in separately using email codes or Google. An administrator-approved adult–athlete link grants access; typing a child's name must never grant access. Two guardians can access the same child, and one guardian can access several children. Use a many-to-many relationship rather than fixed mother/father columns.

The dashboard shows each linked athlete, charges by month, outstanding amount, and payments awaiting review. Payment instructions display the association's confirmed bank/SINPE destination, the amount, and the required athlete name in the transfer description. For a transfer covering siblings, request both names.

After making the transfer in their bank app, the parent first uploads a JPEG, PNG or PDF slip. An owned draft preserves the original privately. Optional reading suggests untouched fields; unknown fields remain manual, and corrections survive later reads or file changes. The parent reviews amount/date/reference and actual payer name, then explicitly selects the athlete(s) and month(s). Manual native-form submission remains available without JavaScript. The bank reference is text: long references can exceed safe numeric precision or contain leading zeros. The selected athlete is explicit even when the screenshot description is incomplete. The payer may be someone other than the logged-in adult.

Uploads show `Pendiente de revisión`; they do not reduce the outstanding balance. Parents may supply additional information when requested. Store revisions instead of silently replacing submitted evidence. The MVP enforces a 3 MB limit, validates permitted file signatures and sizes server-side, and keeps storage private. JPG/PNG originals have a protected enlarged view; PDFs have a browser-rendered first-page view and full-original download. A derived image for PDF reading remains separate from the original evidence.

**Administrator experience.** Provide views for pending submissions, verified payments, and outstanding monthly charges. Review shows the slip alongside the entered fields and proposed allocation. The administrator independently checks the receiving bank account and confirms reference, amount, date, currency, and destination; the image itself is not proof of funds received.

Available actions are verify, request information, or reject with a parent-visible reason. Verification stores the actual reconciled bank details, verifier, timestamp, and allocation to charges. An authenticated administrator may also record a bank-verified payment on behalf of a parent, with an audit reason, when the family cannot use the portal. Store the recording user separately from the designated authorized adult responsible for the payment/receipt and any unallocated credit; for an admin-entered payment, require that adult to be selected explicitly.

Verification creates a unique receipt such as `AB-2026-000123`, saves its immutable data, and queues email in one database transaction. Repeated requests or two administrators approving concurrently must produce one accepted payment and one receipt. Use database constraints and conditional transitions, not only a disabled button. D1 supports transactional statement batches; validate that the approval implementation rolls back on failed invariants. [D1 batch API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/worker-api/d1-database/).

Keep payment review, charge balance, receipt status, and email delivery status separate. A verified payment stays verified when email fails. Admins see failed delivery and can retry. Corrections to verified records require an audited reversal/void and replacement where appropriate; keep historical numbers and snapshots.

**Monthly charges.** Generate charges per active athlete and billing month, due on the eighth day using America/Costa_Rica. Make generation idempotent so retries do not duplicate charges. For the first version, an administrator previews and publishes each month's charges. Snapshot the applicable rate and any explicit discount/waiver; future fee changes must not rewrite prior months. The user confirmed manually entered discounts until the precise sibling rule is clarified. Existing arrears, if imported, are entered as documented opening charges rather than inferred.

One transfer can cover several athletes/months, and several transfers can pay one charge. Support partial payment allocation from the outset. A verified overpayment remains explicitly recorded as unallocated credit until an admin applies or resolves it; it must not disappear. Outstanding amounts derive from charges, adjustments and verified allocations, with pending claims displayed separately.

**Receipt.** Issue one numbered association receipt per verified transfer. Include association identity, receipt number, payer, payment date, issue date, CRC amount, bank reference, and line items showing each athlete, month and amount credited. Show any unallocated amount separately. Save these values at issuance so later profile changes do not alter an old receipt.

For the MVP, send a styled HTML receipt and an authenticated link to a print-friendly copy that supports the browser's Save as PDF. A server-generated PDF attachment can follow if the association requires one; benchmark that generation within Workers Free before promising it. The receipt acknowledges the association's verified payment and is scoped here as an association receipt.

Latest user clarification (11 October): email the bank-confirmed receipt only to the original verified uploader for a self-service family submission. Do not send automatic copies to other adults. Staff-assisted submissions email the family’s designated adult from the authorized stored account record. If the recipient is inactive or its stored email is missing/invalid, delivery is held for review; no arbitrary address is substituted. Core missing-account and guardian-link checks still fail closed. Historical shared-document permissions remain separate from future email routing. A guardian linked to only one child in a multi-child payment should receive only that child's payment history, not unrelated children's details. Default raw bank-slip access to the submitting parent and administrators; sharing a child's balance does not automatically share another adult's bank evidence. For admin-entered evidence, explicitly record whether it can be shared with the designated adult.

**Minimum relational model.**

| Record | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `users` | Auth-provider identity, verified contact information, application role/status. |
| `athletes` | Athlete identity, active state, start month, billing category/rate. |
| `guardian_links`, `invitations` | Explicit adult–athlete access, notification preference, secure expiring invitations. |
| `charges`, `charge_adjustments` | Athlete/month obligation, due date, rate snapshot, documented discounts/waivers/corrections. |
| `payments`, `payment_files` | Submitted and verified transfer details, recording user, designated authorized adult, evidence revisions, review state and private file keys. |
| `slip_drafts`, `slip_extractions`, OCR budgets | Owned temporary uploads, separately validated suggestions/source hashes, and reserved reading attempts. |
| `payment_allocations` | Amount of a verified transfer applied to each charge; unapplied amount remains explicit. |
| `receipts` | Unique sequence/number, frozen receipt data, issued/void status. |
| `audit_events`, `email_outbox` | Change history and independently retryable notifications. |

Store amounts as integer minor units with currency `CRC`; never floating point. Distinguish transfer time, submission time, verification time, and receipt issue time. Match normalized bank references within the relevant bank/payment method/receiving account. Flag duplicates and prevent the same real transfer being credited twice. A file hash is a useful warning, not a substitute for bank matching; duplicate warnings must not reveal another family's data.

**Access and operations.** Enforce guardian links and administrator permissions on every server request, including file downloads, receipt access and exports. Use secure HTTP-only sessions, CSRF protection, rate limits and private/no-store responses for personal records. Collect only needed child information and keep screenshots out of logs, public assets and Git.

Use hosted WorkOS AuthKit and its server-side sessions. Place staff into an administrator organization with required TOTP MFA; admin operations require both the expected authenticated organization context and an active application admin role. A normal parent session for the same person must not bypass MFA. The first technical spike must verify Astro 7 compatibility, Workers execution, session refresh and this authorization boundary. Official SDK availability is not an end-to-end integration test. [Astro SDK](https://github.com/workos/authkit-astro), [MFA](https://workos.com/docs/authkit/mfa), [organization policies](https://workos.com/docs/authkit/organization-policies).

Keep an application-owned audit log in D1, a CSV export for administration, and a documented restore procedure. D1 Free has seven days of point-in-time recovery; arrange regular independent database/evidence exports with restricted access and test a restoration before launch. Assign the association ownership of provider accounts, recovery access and domain renewal. [D1 recovery limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/).

**Implementation sequence.**

1. Validate the stack with synthetic data: Astro + Workers, WorkOS parent login/admin MFA, a D1 write, private R2 upload/download, printable receipt and test email. Measure CPU and confirm production free-feature eligibility before committing.
2. Implement adult invitations, athlete enrollment/links, monthly charge preview/publication, mobile upload, and parent history. Use explicit manual discount amounts until the sibling rule is confirmed.
3. Complete administrator reconciliation, transactional approval, allocation, numbered receipts, reversals, email throttling/retry, outstanding balance views, and CSV/export/restore operations.
4. Pilot with a few families for one monthly cycle, then invite the remainder after the association verifies balances and receipt wording.

Acceptance checks should cover unrelated-family access denial; two guardians on one athlete; revoked access; a long bank reference; one transfer across siblings; partial payments; overpayments; duplicate references; simultaneous approval; requested corrections; email outage/quota exhaustion; no admin-MFA bypass; receipt stability after profile changes; and restoration of database and files. These are material correctness checks for payment records, rather than broad UI snapshot tests.

**Optional OCR is implemented locally.** Original bank evidence and every submitted revision remain unchanged; extraction is separate, editable assistance. `OCR_MODE=off` preserves manual entry, `demo` returns fixed fictitious data for local synthetic accounts without reading the file or calling Groq, and `groq` requires a real WorkOS session, server key and explicit reading action. Groq requests are bounded to 50/day globally, 10/user/day, two/minute and one in flight; successful unchanged-source results are reused. These controls do not guarantee provider-wide free usage. Admin review displays suggestions separately and preserves independent bank verification. PDFs use only a bounded first-page conversion for reading while retaining the complete original. Temporary draft cleanup excludes financial evidence. Actual provider/model access, recognition accuracy and the new OCR/zoom/PDF browser interaction remain unverified. Configuration, limits and staging checks are in [the OCR implementation note](docs/PAYSLIP-OCR.md).

Defer automatic bank reconciliation, bank credentials/API integrations, card payments, SMS/WhatsApp automation, automatic late fees and fiscal invoicing.

**Remaining product decisions.** Confirm whether the 25% sibling discount applies to every sibling or selected fees, and how scholarships/proration work. Confirm who may receive shared receipts, the association's official receipt identity/details, existing arrears to import, evidence retention, and whether an existing sending domain/billing account is available. These do not prevent prototyping the core flow with explicit fees and synthetic data.
